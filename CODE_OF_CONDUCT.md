# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome questions from contributors of all experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback and accept it graciously.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, and persistent disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer using the contact options available through the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
